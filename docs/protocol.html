The four current methods share initialization, minibatch stream and resampling quantiles. Historical controls preserve their original checkpoints/draw streams; they are version diagnostics rather than single-factor ablations. SCANA-R's zero-scale branches preserve source coverage but are not new recovery experience. The calibrated error bank and recovery bank are fixed across final policy seeds.

The frozen JSON files retain original source hashes for provenance. They do not assert that refactored release filenames have those original hashes. See ../provenance/source_files.json for the release mapping.
