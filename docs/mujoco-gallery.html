SCANA-R project

Eight-task MuJoCo study

11,600 earlier closed-loop executions · Six additional manipulation tasks · Frozen-policy deployment tests

VideosResultsProtocol & reproduction

Expanded testing reveals limits. Across six Meta-World tasks, SCANA-R achieves 89.5% nominal macro success versus 91.8% for Clean repeat. The paired difference is −2.33 points, 95% interval [−5.00, 0.17]. New ACT layouts reproduce benefits over Clean, but stress tests expose failures. All outcomes are retained.

These are actual MuJoCo executions of task-specific state policies. This study does not evaluate a language planner, a visual VLA or hardware. Meta-World uses a disclosed 250-step imitation protocol, not the official MT10/ML10 benchmark.

Video provenance · Exact collection and evaluation protocol

Six task comparisons

Five policy seeds × 20 layouts per task/method/condition. Right-hand success occurs after the execution pulse; it cannot be satisfied solely before the intervention. The methods use current privileged state and a fixed recovery bank per configuration.

6,000 rollout records · Paired contrasts · Acquisition costs

Replanning and deployment stress

Forty frozen ACT checkpoints, two tasks and twenty fresh layouts; no retraining. More frequent action replanning does not automatically improve these chunk policies. Late-pulse success is only 0–1% across methods.

5,600 rollout records · Paired contrasts

Simulation success, successful collection and safe physical execution are different claims. The study validates recorded simulated interactions and their task outcomes; it does not establish collision safety or real-robot transfer. Data, code, frozen hashes and failures remain available in the local release.
